# plan-goal-fit

Stop multi-session plans from passing every gate while their sessions do not reach the operator's goal, and stop `/v-pm` splitting small tasks into many sessions.

## Learned
- No surveyed framework measures how often plans reach their goal, and all of them check the goal with a model. A script count of goal-case ownership is the new part.
- Agents that succeed 22% of the time predicted 77%, so the plan states failure paths, never a percentage (https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06948).
- File count is the wrong session size: a givore.com small change touches 70 files. Size counts brief rows, and a glob row counts once.
- `checks/master-SC-4.sh` and `checks/stack-packs-SC-7.sh` already exit 1 on `0ac19f3`, on refusals of their own plans.
- 7 unit tests fail on `0ac19f3` and on S1 alike: `document-standard`, `plan-probes`, `research-clarify`, `sandbox-probe` and `test-hooks-tools-rename`.
- Under `set -e`, a helper whose final `while` loop returns 1 makes `gate.sh` exit 1 with no refusal line; `pm-plan.sh check` then printed `pm: ok`.

## Behaviors & rules
- A plan without `goal_fit: required` → `gate.sh goal` prints one note and exits 0.
- An opted-in plan with a goal case whose owner is empty, dropped, or `operator` without a `## Open & deferred` mention → `gate.sh goal` exits 1 naming `owned N/M`.
- A goal case count outside `^[1-9][0-9]{0,8}$` → refused; edge: `08` and a 20-digit count are refused, never wrapped.
- A runtime estimate above the limit, compared unrounded in minutes → refused.
- A `todo` scope holding a deferral phrase of `templates/master-plan.md` → `gate.sh master` exits 1; edge: a phrase followed by `for <id>` naming a todo or doing session passes, and done rows are skipped.
- Two consecutive todo sessions of one repo whose brief rows sum to at most `session_max_changes` → refused unless one scope has `split: <reason>`.
- A sub-gate that exits nonzero without a `REFUSED` line → `pm-plan.sh check` refuses.
