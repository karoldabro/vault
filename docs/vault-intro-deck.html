1 / 8 · the problem

Your AI forgets everything.

Every new chat starts from zero. You explain the project again, the decisions again, the gotchas again. And whatever it figured out yesterday is gone.

We gave ours a memory. Here's how.

2 / 8 · the vault

A folder of notes the AI keeps.

Plain-text notes that live right in the project: what we decided and why, what's been done, how things fit together. The AI reads them before it starts and updates them when it's done.

vault/
├── decisions/  why we chose things
├── sessions/   what's been done, day by day
└── features/   how each part works

You never maintain these notes. The AI does. And since they sit in the repo, the whole team's AI shares one memory.

3 / 8 · see it once

Ask, and it already knows.

> /v-ask why do we soft-delete orders?
Decided in March: legal needs 90 days of order history.
Full reasoning in decision note #12.

One command. It pulled that from the team's notes and didn't change a thing.

4 / 8 · the commands

Four commands. Two questions.

Just asking?

/v-ask

It looks things up in the notes and never edits anything.

Making a change?

small/v-do

It skips the questions and gets straight to work. It writes notes only if you ask.

normal/v-work

It asks you before doing anything, then writes down what it learned. Not sure the job is small? Pick this one. It spots small jobs and skips the ceremony on its own.

big / risky/v-team

A panel of AI critics argues about the plan before any work starts, and drafts the test plan too. It costs about twice as much, so save it for decisions that would hurt to undo.

5 / 8 · work or team?

Same flow, extra critics.

/v-work

The everyday choice. One AI makes a plan, you say go, it does the job and takes notes. Fine for almost everything.

/v-team

The same flow, plus a panel of AI critics. They pick the plan apart from different angles until they agree, and after the work they review the changes too.

Rule of thumb: if undoing the decision would cost an afternoon, use /v-work. If it would cost a month, use /v-team: database layouts, login and permissions, billing, anything other systems depend on. It costs about twice as much, so don't pay double for a typo.

6 / 8 · the point

Tuesday decides. Thursday remembers.

Tuesday

You run /v-work on exports, and along the way the team settles on CSV over Excel. The AI writes down why, on its own.

Thursday · new chat

A colleague asks /v-ask why CSV? and has the answer in seconds. You didn't have to explain a thing, and their session builds on yours.

That's the whole trick: what one session learns, every later session knows.

7 / 8 · why bother

What you get back.

Less re-explaining.

The AI finds past decisions in the notes instead of digging through the code. On this repo that came out to about 96% less reading.

Decisions survive.

The "why" behind choices stops living in one person's head.

New teammates start smart.

Their AI already knows the project on day one.
